Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

ForumFlow ("the App", "we", "us") is a third-party application available on the Wix App Market, developed by Michael Yadin. The App provides a community forum widget that Wix site owners ("Site Owners") can install on their Wix sites for use by their site visitors and members ("End Users").

2. What Data We Collect

When End Users interact with a ForumFlow-powered forum, we collect and store the following data:

  • Wix Member ID — a unique identifier assigned by Wix to each logged-in user, used to associate posts with their author.
  • Display name — the member's profile name as provided by Wix (nickname or first name), displayed next to their forum posts.
  • Forum content — thread titles, thread bodies, and replies submitted by users.
  • Site instance ID — a Wix identifier that scopes all forum data to the specific site on which the App is installed.
  • Timestamps — date and time of post creation.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, payment information, or any sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To display forum threads and replies on the site where the App is installed.
  • To attribute posts to their authors using display names.
  • To enforce site-specific permissions set by the Site Owner (e.g., members-only posting).
  • To allow users to delete their own posts.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the forum.

4. Data Storage & Security

Forum data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase (Frankfurt region, EU). Access to the database requires a secret service-role key that is never exposed to the browser or widget. All communication between the widget and our servers occurs over HTTPS. Row-Level Security (RLS) is enabled on the database.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The only sub-processor we use is Supabase for database hosting. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

6. Data Retention

Forum posts are retained as long as the App is installed on the site. If a Site Owner uninstalls ForumFlow, their site's data may be deleted upon request. Individual users may delete their own posts at any time through the forum interface.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies & Tracking

ForumFlow does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts. User identity is established through Wix's built-in authentication tokens — no separate login or cookie is set by our App.

9. Children's Privacy

ForumFlow is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data through the App, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Privacy contact
For privacy-related requests or questions, email us at: support@forumflow.app
