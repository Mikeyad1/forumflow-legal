Privacy Policy

CloudSlash3 · Last updated: June 2026

CloudSlash3 develops Wix apps for site owners. This Privacy Policy applies to all apps we publish on the Wix App Market:

ForumFlow Last Updated Sync

1. Who we are

CloudSlash3 is an independent app developer for the Wix platform. Contact: cloudslash3@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

Each app collects only the data necessary to operate its core functionality:

  • ForumFlow — Wix site instance ID, member display names (from Wix), forum posts and replies, timestamps.
  • Last Updated Sync — Wix site instance ID, timestamps of site update events, widget display preferences, sync history logs.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, payment information, or any sensitive personal data.

3. How we use your data

Data is used solely to provide the app's functionality to the site where it is installed. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

4. Data storage and security

Data is stored on secure third-party infrastructure (Supabase / Render.com). All communication between our apps and our servers occurs over HTTPS. We do not expose secret keys or credentials to the browser.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. The only sub-processors we use are our hosting providers (Supabase, Render.com, Cloudflare) for the purpose of running the apps.

6. Data retention

Data is retained as long as the app is installed on your site. You may request deletion of your site's data at any time by contacting us at cloudslash3@gmail.com.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Contact us at cloudslash3@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and tracking

Our apps do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts. User identity is established through Wix's built-in authentication — no separate login or cookie is set by our apps.

9. Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
